About Us

Discover the story, vision, and mission behind Johannesburg's premier high-tech gaming and esports venue.

Our History

Established in 2023 in Johannesburg, Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is an initiative founded by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer. He noticed that many local gamers lacked access to high-performance gaming equipment and organized competitive events. To address this, he established a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy cutting-edge technology, compete in esports tournaments, and socialize with friends. Over the years, we have expanded to become a core hub for school leagues, professional tournament hosting, and community gaming events.

Our Vision

To cultivate and lead South Africa's premier gaming culture, bridging structural gaps in local esports development while fostering safe, accessible, and high-performance gaming environments for families and youth alike.

Our Mission

We deliver state-of-the-art interactive gaming infrastructure combined with structured, community-centered experiences. We design customized packages tailored for parties, institutions, and team-building scenarios to guarantee unforgettable memories.

Our Goals

  1. Establish professional esports training courses for regional high schools and youth clubs.
  2. Provide accessible premium hardware options enabling low-barrier local talent incubation.
  3. Build strategic B2B pipelines to host world-class collaborative corporate team-building outings.
  4. Maintain a robust calendar of community-centric competitive weekly events in Gauteng.
